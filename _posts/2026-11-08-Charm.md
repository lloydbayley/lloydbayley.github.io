A lovely, clever and indispensable app for apps that refuse to conform to the native Apple spelling, autocorrect and grammar checker.\

As the website says "Just type. Charm handles the rest. A private writing assistant that
runs entirely on your Mac. Buy once, own it forever, no subscription."\

[https://www.theodorehq.com/charm/](https://www.theodorehq.com/charm/){:target="_blank"}

I searched for a solution to the fact that the new ChatGPT desktop app for the mac had no autocorrect or prediction available, which make no sense to me because the classic version has it. Why would you bring out a new version and remove such a feature?

Charm works perfectly and allows you to select which features you want to have on a per-app basis. 

You have the ability to choose from a local, integrated LLM (Local Language Model) or a one-time LLM download of two different categories.

The app makes sure you understand that it runs completely locally and once the download is completed, no data is sent back anywhere on the internet.

A one-off payment of $19.99 with a 14 day trial/refund offer was an excellent offer.

I had a question for the developers and upon sending an email, it was promptly-answered. The very quick response time and efficient and friendly service was another plus-factor for me to make the purchase.

If you want total local control over your spelling/grammar/autocorrect/text-prediction, I suggest you use Charm.
